Moral Skeletons

What 38 films treat as the source of legitimate authority, who they punish, and whose inner life they grant — read out of the films' own text.

What this is, and what it is not

Every number here comes from a moral skeleton: a structured extraction run over each film's plot, critical reception and full dialogue track. That part is real.

There are no factor scores yet. The item bank has not been built, nothing has been scored against it, and the factor analysis has never run — so the emergent dimensions do not exist and nothing here is a position on one. What follows are the structural signals the skeletons contain, which are the inputs to that analysis rather than its output.

01 · Unprompted

The axis, recovered rather than imposed

The extraction was never told about a Given Order ↔ Self-Authorship spectrum. It was asked one open question — what does this film treat as the source of legitimate authority? — and answered in free text. These are three of those answers, verbatim.

03 · The hard problem

Depiction is not endorsement

A plot summary of Starship Troopers reads as a sincere war picture; the film is a satire of one. Nine films were flagged as presenting conduct they are actually critiquing. This is the check that a naive reading fails, so it is worth seeing which films tripped it.

05 · Honest gaps

What is missing

  • No item bank. Propositions were harvested from only 6 of 38 films, all released 1939–1953, so the vocabulary would be badly era-skewed. Re-running across the whole corpus comes first.
  • No scores, so no dimensions. Until films are scored against a bank there is nothing to factor-analyse, and the axes stay a hypothesis. The single question that decides the interface design — whether the top two factors explain enough spread to justify a 2-D map instead of bars — is unanswered.
  • Two films short. 38 of 40 carry a full skeleton; Wicked has no subtitle track in either OPUS release, and one other did not complete.
  • Every film here is famous. The extraction was instructed hard against answering from memory, and the median film names 2 fields it could not ground — evidence the discipline is holding. It is not proof. Obscure titles would settle it.
